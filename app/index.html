The road to the platinum — 6 phases

  1. Phase 1 — Story. Play Chapters 1–4 and the final mission in order. Story trophies (#2–#13, #7) pop as you go; keep an eye out for the missable set-pieces in #37 and #45 while you are there.
  2. Phase 2 — Strangers & Freaks. Work the stranger strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret) as their markers appear. Eight finished strands earns the silver; do Bret's hunt (#29) before the story ends.
  3. Phase 3 — Activities & skill. Sample all nine activity types, then grind the counts: 250 stunts, 20 race wins, drone races at every location, gold medals at the Shooting Range, fishing, arcade, bowling and meditation.
  4. Phase 4 — Collectibles & exploration. Card sets, 100 WAiNK spots, 100 vehicles, and every corner of Leonida — sweep the map region by region and let the counters build.
  5. Phase 5 — Money & crime. Launder $250,000 of Marked Bills, spend $100,000 in shops, buy every garage, and run the robberies: jewelry store, armored truck, five bank vaults, one illegal operation per gang.
  6. Phase 6 — Duo feats & 100%. Partner trophies with Lucia/Jason, then mop up whatever the checklist shows as missing until the in-game counter reads 100% — A Long Road pops, and the platinum follows.
